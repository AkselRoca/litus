Le mockup photographique principal a été produit avec le mode intégré ImageGen à partir d’une capture réelle du site et du symbole Litus. Prompt final :

> Edit this photographic mockup into a WIDE PANORAMIC image, output 1792x768 pixels (aspect 2.33:1). Preserve the actual West Clôtures website design and all branding. Reframe the SAME desk scene to SHOW ENTIRE laptop including top edge, screen, keyboard and base, with margin above and below; laptop should occupy central 60% of image width and 82% of image height, in subtle natural perspective like a portfolio photograph. Laptop centered horizontally at 52%. White mug with orange/black Litus SYMBOL ONLY to the far right. Far left 18% warm pale cream office negative space with blurred plant. Desk spans entire bottom. Warm realistic natural window light. No lettering on mug, no added UI, no annotations, no banners, no page text. It is critical that image is a wide horizontal photograph and the laptop is fully visible, never cropped.
